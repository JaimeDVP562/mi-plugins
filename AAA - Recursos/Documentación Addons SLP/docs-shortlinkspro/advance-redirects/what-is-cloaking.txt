<h1>What is URL Cloaking?</h1>

URL cloaking (also known as link masking or link framing) is a technique that allows you to display content from another website while keeping your own domain visible in the visitor's browser address bar.

<h2>How URL Cloaking Works</h2>

When you create a cloaked link with ShortLinks Pro:

<ol>
<li>Your server loads an HTML page from your domain</li>
<li>This page contains a full-size iframe that displays the destination website</li>  
<li>The visitor's browser continues to display your domain in the address bar</li>
<li>The content they see comes from the destination website</li>
</ol>

<h2>Benefits of URL Cloaking</h2>

<h3>Brand Protection</h3>

Maintain your brand presence throughout the user experience by keeping your domain visible.

<h3>Affiliate Marketing</h3>

Present affiliate offers while maintaining your domain as the visible URL, creating a more seamless experience and potentially improving conversion rates.

<h3>Traffic Control</h3>

Technically, visitors remain on your domain, which can:
<ul>
<li>Reduce bounce rates in analytics</li>
<li>Allow for easier return to your content</li>
<li>Enable additional tracking opportunities</li>
</ul>

<h3>Link Continuity</h3>

If a destination URL changes, you can update the cloaked link without changing the URL your audience uses.

<h2>Important Considerations</h2>

<h3>Compatibility</h3>

Some websites block being displayed in iframes for security reasons, which can prevent cloaking from working properly.

<h3>Transparency</h3>

While cloaking is useful for many legitimate purposes, it's important to maintain transparency with your audience about where links lead.

<h3>Mobile Experience</h3>

On mobile devices, the framed experience may not always display optimally depending on the target site's mobile responsiveness.

<h2>Setting Up Cloaked Redirects</h2>

To create a cloaked redirect with the Advanced Redirects add-on:

<ol>
<li>Edit or create a link in ShortLinks Pro</li>
<li>Select "Cloaked" from the Redirect Type dropdown</li>
<li>Configure the title and optional custom CSS</li>
<li>Save your link</li>
</ol>
