In this guide, you'll learn how to configure a link to expire on a specific date.

If you want to configure the expiration condition based on clicks or unique clicks refer to the other configuration section.

<h2> Configure the link expiration date </h2>

<b>1</b> - Go to your prefered link and click <b>Edit</b>.

[Captura: configure1.png]

<b>2</b> - Then go to in the <b>"Expiration"</b> section, click on "No" and select "Date".

[Captura: configure_date1.png]

<b>3</b> - <b>Two date fields</b> and one for the <b>expired URL</b> will now appear. In the first one, enter the <b>year, month, and day</b>.

[Captura: configure_date2.png]

<b>4</b> - In the next field, enter the <b>hour and minute</b> you want the link to expire, you can even put the hour you are in.

[Captura: configure_date3.png]

<b>5</b> - In the last field, enter the <b>URL</b> that the original link will <b>redirect to</b> once it expires.

[Captura: configure_date4.png]

<b>6</b> - Click <b>Update</b> to save your changes.

[Captura: configure2.png]

And that's it! Your link will now expire on the specified date and redirect to the new URL — if you provided one.
