In this guide, you'll learn how to configure a link to expire after a specific number of clicks or unique clicks.

If you want to configure the expiration condition based on a specific date refer to the other configuration section.

<h2> Configure Link Expiration Based on Clicks </h2>

<b>1</b> - Go to your preferred link and click <b>Edit</b>.

[Captura: configure1.png]

<b>2</b> - Then go to in the <b>"Expiration"</b> section, click on "No" and select "Clicks" or "Unique Clicks".

[Captura: configure_clicks1.png]

<b>3</b> - A field for the <b>number of clicks</b> and one for the <b>expired URL</b> will now appear. In the first field, enter the <b>number of clicks</b> or <b>unique clicks</b>.

[Captura: configure_clicks2.png]

<b>4</b> - In the last field, enter the <b>URL</b> that the original link will <b>redirect to</b> once it expires.

[Captura: configure_clicks3.png]

<b>5</b> - Click <b>Update</b> to save your changes.

[Captura: configure2.png]

And that's it! Your link will now expire on the specified number of clicks or unique clicks, and redirect to the new URL — if you provided one.
