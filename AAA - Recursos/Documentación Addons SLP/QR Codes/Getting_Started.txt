<h1>QR Codes</h1>

The QR Code add-on in ShortLinks Pro allows you to attach QR codes to your links and customize their appearance.
All options are available under the <strong>QR Codes</strong> tab on settings.

[Captura: configure_qr1.png]

To see these options, you must have the PHP library <strong>Imagick</strong> or <strong>GD</strong> installed. However, GD only supports QR code sizing.


<h2>QR Codes Configuration</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Size</strong> – Enter the desired pixel size for your QR code. Default value is 500px.

[Captura: configure_qr2.png]

<li><strong>Error Correction Level</strong> – Choose between Low, Medium, Quartile, or High. Higher levels increase the chance of successful scanning, 
even with damage or distortion, but also make the code more dense.

[Captura: configure_qr3.png]

<li><strong>Color Mode</strong> – You can choose between two visual styles:
    <ul>
        <li>Solid – One single foreground color.</li>
        <li>Gradient – Two colors blended together for a gradient effect.</li>
    </ul>
</li>

[Captura: configure_qr4.png]

<li><strong>Foreground Color</strong> – This is the primary color of the QR code.

[Captura: configure_qr5.png]

<li><strong>Foreground Color 2</strong> – Only visible when using Gradient mode. Select the second color to complete the gradient.

[Captura: configure_qr6.png]

<li><strong>Custom Eye Colors</strong> – Toggle this switch ON to manually pick colors for the corner "eyes" of the QR code.

[Captura: configure_qr7.png]

<li><strong>Background Color</strong> – Pick a background color that fits your brand or design.</li>

[Captura: configure_qr8.png]
</ul>

<li><strong>Configured Example</strong> – You can save it and see the ending result when you edit a link or by pressing "QR Codes" in manage links. 
You also have the option to download them!.

[Captura: configure_qr9.png][Captura: configure_qr9_2.png]


All these settings are global and will apply to QR codes created for future links!.
