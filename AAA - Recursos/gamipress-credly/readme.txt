Sync achievements and user earnings with Credly.

== Description ==

Open badges are verifiable, portable digital badges with embedded metadata about skills and achievements created by the Mozilla fundation.

Credly is a digital credentials platform that lets you issue open badges as an official recognition of the skills of your users.

This add-on lets you connect your GamiPress achievements with Credly and make your website achievements shareable and open badge compatible.

= Features =

* Sync any achievement of your choice with Credly.
* Make your site achievements Open Badge compatible.
* Issue achievements automatically on Credly when user earns an synced achievement.
* Import all badges you already have on Credly to your site.
* Option to auto-sync your users accounts with Credly.
* Block to place a Credly sync form to let users connects his account with Credly.
* Shortcode to place the Credly sync form anywhere (with support to GamiPress live shortcode embedder).
* Widget to place the Credly sync form on any sidebar.
* New fields and attributes on GamiPress Achievements block, shortcode and widget to display only achievements synced.

== Installation ==

= From WordPress backend =

1. Navigate to Plugins -> Add new.
2. Click the button "Upload Plugin" next to "Add plugins" title.
3. Upload the downloaded zip file and activate it.

= Direct upload =

1. Upload the downloaded zip file into your `wp-content/plugins/` folder.
2. Unzip the uploaded zip file.
3. Navigate to Plugins menu on your WordPress admin area.
4. Activate this plugin.
